01 · Company

We are not just a trader. We are an integrated importer, present at the origin and organised for European industry.

For decades, our family has been collecting and exporting acacia gum in Chad and Sudan. Détails Import, founded in France, puts this field expertise at the service of European food, pharmaceutical, cosmetic and nutrition manufacturers.

In Chad, our team runs collection, field quality control, stock and export. In France, we handle management, business development, import logistics and customer relations.
