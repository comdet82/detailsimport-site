Our story

Decades in the field, a European company.

For decades, our family has been collecting and exporting acacia gum in Chad and Sudan. This history has given us an intimate knowledge of the land, the producers and the natural harvest cycles.

Ali Manssour, who grew up in Chad, studied in France and founded Détails Groupe there, an entrepreneurial group active in architecture, construction and business services. Meanwhile, the family carried on collecting, storing and exporting gum in Chad.

Détails Import is where these two worlds meet. The company brings together four siblings with complementary skills, Ali, Makka, Abdoulaye and Imane, combining the family's expertise in the African supply chain with technical skills developed in Europe: quality, pharmacy, engineering and entrepreneurship.

At the foot of the tree

Harvested, sorted and dried by hand.

It all starts on the acacia tree. Harvesters make fine incisions in the bark; the sap flows and hardens in the sun into gum tears, which they then pick by hand.

The gum is collected from our partner producers, then sorted by hand and by machine according to grade, colour and size. It is cleaned, naturally sun-dried and checked at our Ndjari site in N'Djamena.

Every step is documented: this is how we guarantee the traceability of every lot all the way to your plant.
