Cosmetics

A natural ingredient for demanding formulas.

In cosmetics, acacia gum is listed under the INCI name Acacia Senegal Gum. It forms a light film on skin and hair, stabilises emulsions and gives body to textures.

It meets the growing demand for naturally derived ingredients: it is a tree exudate, 100% plant-based, with no animal derivatives, hand-harvested and sun-dried.

For brands, raw material traceability has become a selling point in its own right. We document every lot from harvest in Chad to delivery.

Choosing a form

Raw or spray-dried: which product for your laboratory?

Spray-dried gum disperses easily in the water phase and suits most formulations. Raw gum is for manufacturers who prepare their own solutions or extracts.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the INCI name of acacia gum?
Acacia Senegal Gum (CAS 9000-01-5).
What are its functions in cosmetics?
Film-forming agent, emulsion stabiliser, texturiser, binder and hair fixative.
Is acacia gum suitable for vegan formulas?
It is a 100% plant-based ingredient with no animal derivatives, so it can be used in plant-based formulas. The claim made on the finished product (vegan or other) depends on the standard chosen by the brand and remains its responsibility.
What about animal testing?
In the European Union, animal testing is banned for finished cosmetic products and their ingredients, and placing animal-tested cosmetics on the market has been banned since 2013 (Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009).
What traceability do you offer?
Every lot is documented from harvest in Chad to delivery, tested by the Eurofins laboratory and delivered with its certificate of analysis, technical data sheet and safety data sheet.
