Pharmaceutical

Purity, traceability and consistent specifications.

In pharmacy, acacia gum has long been used as an excipient: it binds powders, keeps particles in suspension and forms coating films.

In this sector, consistency from one lot to the next matters as much as purity. That is why every lot we deliver is tested by Eurofins and comes with its certificate of analysis, and why our quality is overseen by Abdoulaye Mansour, doctor of pharmacy.

Our specifications follow the requirements of the European Pharmacopoeia; the related documentation is available on request for your supplier qualification files.

Choosing a form

Raw or spray-dried: which product for your formulations?

Spray-dried gum is the easiest to use in granulation and solution preparation. Raw gum lets laboratories with their own dissolution and purification process start from an unprocessed raw material.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is acacia gum used for in pharmacy?
It is an excipient: a binder in tablets, a suspending agent and emulsifier in syrups, a coating agent and a base for lozenges. It is also used to microencapsulate active ingredients.
What testing do you carry out on each lot?
Every lot is tested by the independent laboratory Eurofins: moisture, ash, pH, viscosity and microbiological testing (total aerobic count, absence of Salmonella and E. coli).
Who is responsible for quality?
Quality at Détails Import is overseen by Abdoulaye Mansour, doctor of pharmacy and Quality Director.
What documents do you provide?
Lot certificate of analysis, technical data sheet, safety data sheet, phytosanitary and origin certificates, and European Pharmacopoeia documentation on request.
Can you supply samples for supplier qualification?
Yes. We send samples with their documentation for your trials and qualification file.
Is acacia gum animal-derived?
No. It is an acacia exudate, 100% plant-based, with no animal-derived ingredients.
