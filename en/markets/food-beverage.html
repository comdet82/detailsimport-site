Food & beverage

A clean label ingredient, used for millennia.

Acacia gum is the hardened sap of the Sahel acacia tree. Hand-harvested for more than 4,000 years, it is now an authorised food additive in the European Union under the code E414.

Its key property: it dissolves easily in water and remains low in viscosity even at high concentrations. This makes it the reference emulsifier for flavoured beverages and a highly sought-after coating agent in confectionery.

It has no marked taste and no disruptive colour, so it can be added without changing the sensory profile of your recipes.

Choosing a form

Raw or spray-dried: which product for your plant?

For beverages, flavours and powder blends, spray-dried gum dissolves quickly and goes straight into production. Raw gum suits manufacturers who carry out dissolution and filtration themselves, especially in confectionery and winemaking.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is E414 acacia gum?
It is a natural exudate of the acacia tree (Acacia senegal), hand-harvested in the Sahel. In the European Union it is authorised as a food additive under the code E414 and is mainly used as an emulsifier, stabiliser and coating agent.
Which form should I choose for a beverage?
Spray-dried acacia gum powder dissolves quickly and goes straight into production. Raw gum is suitable if you carry out dissolution and filtration yourself.
Is acacia gum plant-based?
Yes. It is 100% plant-based and contains no animal-derived ingredients, so it can be used in plant-based formulations. The final claim on your product remains the manufacturer's responsibility.
Is it gluten-free?
Yes, acacia gum is naturally gluten-free. Our technical data sheets also declare it GMO-free and free from food allergens.
What quality guarantees do you provide?
Détails Import is ISO 22000 certified. Every lot is tested by the independent laboratory Eurofins and delivered with its certificate of analysis, technical data sheet, safety data sheet, and phytosanitary and origin certificates.
What volumes can you deliver?
