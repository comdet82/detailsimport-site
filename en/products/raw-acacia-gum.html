Description

Raw acacia gum comes as natural nodules, white to yellow in colour. It is the original form of the gum, as harvested from the acacia tree, then sorted, cleaned and naturally sun-dried.

It is intended for manufacturers who carry out dissolution, filtration or processing themselves, in food, pharmaceutical and cosmetic applications.
